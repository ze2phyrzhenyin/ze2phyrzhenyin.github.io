# Glossary for the zh and es versions

English is the source. Use these renderings so the parts translated separately read as one document.

## Names that stay as written

- Organisation, company, programme, product, law, regulation and publication names stay in their original form: Ghana Statistical Service, Bank of Ghana, KOBA, The Or Foundation, Jibu, Valmont Gadgets, Badili, Yaga, PEDL, CEPR, GSMA, Act 1124, L.I. 1932, Sale of Goods Act 1962, Consumer Protection Act, TC Daily, StatsBank, GHANEPS, WITS, Food for All Africa, Recycle Up! Ghana. Do not add glosses in brackets.
- Market and neighbourhood names stay in Latin script: Kwame Nkrumah Circle, Circle, Kantamanto, Adum, Aboabo, Alabar, Kejetia, Asafo, Gikomba, Old Fadama, Osu, Nima, Airport, Madina, New Town, Angré, Plateau Dokui, Treichville, Legon, Enchi, Ejisu, Kpone.
- Local and source-language terms stay as written, in their quotes: kayayei, obroni wawu, borla, mitumba, friperie, « occasion », « venu de France », « reconditionné », « troc », « garantie », « propre », Koko, Twi, Ga.
- Identifiers stay exactly: S016, S016-01, C01, E02, R04, O11, HS 630900, HS 851713, GR/TEMA/GDS/26/0017, \*315#.
- Currency codes stay before the number: GHS 400, USD 115.28m, KSh 27.81bn, FCFA 117,000, CFA 42.543bn, R, €, $.
