## English (the version that is spoken)

We help businesses in traditional industries modernize their operations through digital and AI solutions.

Today, many of these businesses still rely on Excel, emails and manual processes. Off-the-shelf software often fails to meet their specific needs.

For example, we developed an intelligent management system for an international electronics warehousing company. It integrates work orders, inventory management, smartphone testing and billing. We even built their own calendar and email system.

It's already in daily use.

However, every business is different, especially in manufacturing, agriculture and logistics. With our growing experience, we can quickly understand complex needs and turn them into simple, intuitive solutions.

But despite the success of this project, some of our client's customers still find learning new software frustrating and time-consuming.

So we want to go one step further.

**What if people no longer had to adapt to software? What if software adapted to them?**

Imagine a retailer placing an order by talking to an AI assistant, a logistics company receiving it through a dashboard, and an accountant continuing to work in Excel.

**Three different interfaces. One connected system.**

Everyone works in their own way, yet they can collaborate seamlessly.

This is the idea behind our new product, which I'm currently developing.

According to France Num's 2026 survey, an estimated 600,000 small and medium-sized businesses in France plan to buy software in 2026 and 2027.

This represents a huge market opportunity for us.

Our vision is to make digital transformation accessible to every business, regardless of its industry or digital skills.

**We believe that in the future, software should adapt to people, not the other way around.**

## 中文原稿

我们通过数字化与 AI 解决方案，帮助传统行业的企业实现运营现代化。

如今，许多传统企业仍然依赖 Excel、电子邮件和人工操作。市面上的标准化软件往往无法满足它们的特定需求。

例如，我们为一家国际电子产品仓储公司开发了一套智能管理系统，集成了工单管理、库存管理、智能手机检测和账单管理等功能。我们甚至为他们开发了专属的日历和电子邮件系统。

目前，这套系统已经投入日常使用。

然而，每家企业都是不同的，尤其是在制造业、农业和物流行业。随着经验的不断积累，我们能够快速理解复杂的需求，并将其转化为简单、直观易用的解决方案。

但是，尽管这个项目非常成功，我们客户的一些客户仍然认为，学习使用新软件是一件令人烦恼且耗费时间的事情。

所以，我们希望更进一步。

**如果人们不再需要适应软件呢？如果软件能够主动适应人呢？**

想象一下，一家零售商通过与 AI 助手对话完成下单，一家物流公司通过管理后台接收订单，而会计人员则继续使用自己熟悉的 Excel。

**三种不同的操作界面，同一个互联的系统。**

每个人都可以按照自己习惯的方式工作，同时实现无缝协作。

这就是我目前正在开发的全新产品背后的核心理念。

根据 France Num 2026 年的调查，预计法国约有60万家中小微企业计划在2026年至2027年期间采购软件。

这为我们带来了巨大的市场机会。

我们的愿景是，无论企业属于什么行业，具备怎样的数字化能力，都能够轻松实现数字化转型。

**我们相信，在未来，软件应该适应人，而不是让人适应软件。**
