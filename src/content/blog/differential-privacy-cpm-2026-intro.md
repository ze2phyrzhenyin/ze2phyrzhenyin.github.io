## An Imperfect Example

In 2023, I met a girl online. We chatted for at least three hours every day, and after two months, she was in a relationship, but I was not. Soon after that, I told a couple at a bar about what had happened to me. And the girl in the couple kept asking me about my school, my major, my hobbies and my sexual orientation. In the end, I wanted to add her boyfriend's WeChat, but she didn't let him add me. She couldn't help asking me, "Are my photos really edited that heavily?"... I ran.

She knew my background, so she could tell from my answers that I was the person she had been talking to late at night. If I had changed a few details, she might not have been able to confirm it.
