Route segments

Medvědín on site → Vrbatova bouda

About 45–55 minutes · Alpine meadows and ridges

From the Medvědín cable car station, follow the marked trail to Vrbatova bouda. The route is dominated by alpine meadows, dwarf pines and open ridges, with the altitude gradually approaching 1,400 m.

Vrbatova bouda → Pančavský vodopád → Ambrožova vyhlídka → Labská bouda

Approx. 50–65 minutes · Waterfall and Labský důl glacier valley

Pančavský vodopád is the highest waterfall in the Czech Republic. Water falls from the edge of the plateau into Labský důl. Ambrožova vyhlídka is located on the upper edge of the valley, overlooking the forest on the valley floor, the slopes on both sides and the edge of the plateau where the waterfall is located. Then continue to Labská bouda.

Labská bouda

Lunch approximately 30–40 minutes · Restaurant, drinks, toilets

Labská bouda is located in an alpine area at an altitude of approximately 1,340 m. Lunch, water and toilet breaks are available here; reservations are available for CZK 300–450 per person.

Labská bouda → Pramen Labe

Approx. 25–30 minutes · The symbolic source of the Elbe

Pramen Labe is the symbolic source site of the Elbe River and features a stone well and city coat of arms wall. The true hydrological source is located within the protected area.

Pramen Labe → U čtyř pánů → Harrachovy kameny → Kotelní jámy → Medvědín

About 2.5–3 hours · Alpine plateaus, granite and cirques

From Pramen Labe to Harrachovy kameny via U čtyř pánů. Granite outcrops on the ridge overlook the surrounding plateau; the Kotelní jámy is a large cirque formed by glacial erosion. Then return to the Medvědín cable car station via Vrbatova bouda.

Route segments

Tři prameny → Pravčická brána

It takes about 45 minutes to go up the mountain and about 30 minutes to go down the mountain.

From the Tři prameny intersection next to the bus station, follow the red signs into the forest, and the second half continues uphill. With a span of approximately 26.5 m, the Plavčice Gate is the longest natural sandstone arch in continental Europe; the Falcon's Nest next to the gate was built in the 19th century. There are several steps to the viewing platform in the toll area, and you cannot go to the top of the stone arch.

Mezní Louka / Mezná Lunch

Approximately 45 minutes · Parking lot and surrounding village

Suitable for dining around the Mezní Louka parking lot, in nearby hotel restaurants or in the village of Mezná. You can usually find Czech dishes, soups, sandwiches and light meals; on Sundays when there are more people, just choose the one nearby.

Mezná → Divoká soutěska

Actual walking time is about 60 minutes · Boat trip is additional

From Mezná, it takes about 20 minutes to go down the steps to Mezní můstek, and then walk upstream of the Kamenice River for about 10 minutes to the lower pier. The narrowest part of the canyon is only accessible by boat; at the end of the boat ride, return to the same side and follow the same path back to Mezná. The road surface was cool and slippery, and the steps on the return trip continued to rise.

Bastei Parkplatz → Main Observation Deck → Stone Bridge

Round trip approximately 40–50 minutes · Paved road and steps

From the near parking lot, walk along the end of Basteistraße to enter the scenic area. First look at the main viewing platform of the Elbe Valley, and then walk to the Bastei Bridge between the sandstone peaks; other viewing spots are subject to on-site opening conditions.

As of August 7, 2026, the main Bastei observation deck and Bastei Bridge are open; the Ferdinand and Pavillion observation decks are closed, and some forest areas between Bastei, Rathen, Hohnstein and Polenztal are closed until August 31. If there are new road closures on site, please refer to the warning lines and official notices.

Equipment, weather and documents

Weather and equipment

  • Current forecast: about 18–24°C in the morning and 29–31°C in the afternoon, mainly sunny to lightly cloudy
  • Prepare 1.5–2 L of water per person, and bring a small amount of salty snacks or fruits
  • Sneakers, trail running shoes, or hiking shoes with normal traction
  • Hat, sunglasses, sunscreen and light jacket
  • Power banks, bank cards and a small amount of CZK / EUR cash
The area has been dry recently and the risk of forest fires is high. Do not smoke, light fires or use open flame stoves in national parks.

Cross-border and car rental documents

  • Original passport
  • All traveling companions must bring original passports or other valid travel documents.
  • Original driver’s license
  • Car rental contract, vehicle registration documents and proof of insurance
  • Written permission or contractual terms from the car rental company to enter Germany
Germany currently implements temporary inspections at the Czech land border. Files should be carried with you, not just cellphone photos.
