Usage notes

How to find a real Lounge

For quiet hotel-style service, prioritise Le 46 or Grand Hotel de l'Opera. For soft lighting and a hidden terrace, consider Sixty-Two. Mahogany is a modern hotel lounge alternative, but DJ nights are not quiet.

How to order neat spirits

At the bar, ask for Armagnac / Cognac by the glass, single cask, independent bottling, cask strength or distillery exclusive. Unlisted stock is marked as "confirm on site".

Jazz & live music

Le Taquin, Le Bijou, Cave Poesie, Le Cri de la Mouette and others — always check the day's programme, tickets and summer closure notices. Event schedules are not fixed year-round opening hours.

View sources and verification notes

Drink responsibly. Never drink and drive. For getting home at night, prefer walking, public transport or licensed ride services.
