Toulouse Bar Recommendations / Source

Data sources and verification instructions

This page corresponds to the address of the main list, regular business hours, wine characteristics, summer suspension, professional list, and the basis for judging "whether it is close to the traditional Whiskey lounge / Speakeasy". Last unified verification date: August 18, 2026.

Source priority:Bar or hotel official website / official wine list / official social homepage ＞ Toulouse Tourism Bureau, City Hall and local program pages of venues ＞ Current professional media, performance platforms and recent business catalogs.

Regarding Lounge judgment:Check “environment” and “wine depth” separately. Leather sofa, dark light, and low volume come from the official description of the venue or recent space information; Cognac, Armagnac, Whiskey, single barrel, and tasting flight are based on the official wine list and tasting page.

Do not solidify Google star rating:Scores continue to change, and sample sizes vary widely. The main page only fixes the verifiable professional rankings, and does not write temporary star ratings into permanent conclusions.

New store observation: Tyrosine · 5 Rue de la Bourse

The address of the original Le 5 Wine Bar is now occupied by Tyrosine. Official information shows that it will open on August 18, 2026, focusing on cheese conveyor belts, artisanal cheeses and wine pairings. Due to the short opening time, it is not ranked alongside bars with stable reputations.

Boundaries and excluded items

27 Rue Gabriel Péri, where L’Apothicaire was originally located, is currently Pompette, so both names are not listed as existing bars. Le 5 Wine Bar has been permanently closed; the new store Tyrosine at the same location is not included in the main recommendation ranking because it has just opened. Public information such as Moloko and Satch Club are shown to be closed or have conflicting statuses, and are not included in the main list.

The summer vacation dates of Le Taquin, Le Bijou, and Au Père Louis are marked separately on the home page; Cave Poésie and Le Cri de la Mouette are "open according to the program" and do not disguise their activity hours as stable business hours.
