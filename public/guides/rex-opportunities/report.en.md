## Materials to prepare

A one-page company profile in French, a three-minute project pitch, a five-minute hands-on demo and a one-page record of actual results.

### Agriculture & food

For: Vinitech · SIVAL · CFIA · FRUIT LOGISTICA · medFEL

Demo: Incoming goods from suppliers or farmers, batches, received weight, stock, sorting or processing, dispatch and documents.

Question: Between goods in and dispatch, at which step is data most often re-entered, or do the quantities fail to match?

Pilot: One product category, one warehouse, one process. Until the farming module is live, label it clearly as a prototype.

### Warehousing & logistics

For: Supply Chain Event · SITL · LogiMAT · transport logistic

Demo: Goods in, product identification, inspection results, locations and stock, orders, goods out and dispatch records.

Question: Which information has to be typed again between Excel, email, the warehouse software and the carrier platforms?

Pilot: One of: order import, generation of shipping documents, linking inspection to stock, or customer look-up of their goods. Equipment, hardware and on-site deployment are scoped separately.

### Talks and startup applications

For: Advanced Factories · WAICF · ENGAGE.EU

Demo: The business problem, the previous process, the functions built, the actual scope of use, measured results and limits.

Question: Which steps are handled automatically, and which must a person confirm?

Pilot: Give separate evidence for internal use, user trials and paying customers; figures that were not measured are not written up as time saved, accuracy or cost results.
