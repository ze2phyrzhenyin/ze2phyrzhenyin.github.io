M01 · CoreAccounts & users

€350

Manage sign-in, accounts and passwords.

Scope

Registration, account maintenance, admin/business/read-only roles and administrator-assisted user switching. Existing authentication only; no SSO, LDAP or MFA redesign. User switching requires authorization, logs and acceptance testing.

3–5 person-daysDevelopment estimate

M02 · CoreRoles & data access

€650

Control what each person can see and do.

Scope

Configure creation, inspection, intake and allocation rights, plus visibility by shop and product category. Customers see authorized data only. Existing scope controls; no multi-tenant SaaS resale. Basic isolation remains mandatory.

5–8 person-daysDevelopment estimate

M03 · CoreShops & reference data

€350

Keep shops, warehouses, product types and addresses organized.

Scope

Manage platforms, shops, product options, warehouses, racks, levels, sales versions, short names and return addresses. Initial data-entry limits are in S01. Maintaining existing shops and warehouses adds no module licence charge.

2–3 person-daysDevelopment estimate

M04 · CoreMulti-category work orders

€700

Create after-sales cases for different products.

Scope

Phones, tablets, computers, watches, earphones, hair dryers, bags, other products and product-free services. Record origin, identifiers, logistics and notes, including assisted entry. Existing fields only; IMEI recording includes no manufacturer lookup, authentication or automatic device reading.

3–5 person-daysDevelopment estimate

M05 · CoreWork-order workflow

€1,400

Track every stage from intake to closure.

Scope

Creation, inspection, intake, allocation, preparation, dispatch, receipt and closure, with progress and history. Product-free services have a separate billing/closure flow. This is the existing workflow skeleton and single-item operations; inspection details, stock, batches and billing require their respective modules. Agree the base route for unpurchased stages.

6–10 person-daysDevelopment estimate

M06Search & bulk actions

€650

Find cases quickly and process several together.

Scope

Overview and personal lists, filters, sorting, case/device lookup, bulk identifier search, allocation, preparation, dispatch, flags and closure. Basic lists are already in M04/M05. Bulk operations require the corresponding business modules.

2–4 person-daysDevelopment estimate
