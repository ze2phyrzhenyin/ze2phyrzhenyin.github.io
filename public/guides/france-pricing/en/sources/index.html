Sources & calculations

SaaS
(Fixed monthly fee + rate × paid users) × 12 × years + setup + annual extras × years. Package prices do not multiply by users.
REX
Uses the quote calculator. Presets include €1,800/year maintenance and €60/month third-party costs.
Person-days
30 modules: 102–170 days, plus 18–30 days of shared work. Estimates come from the reference file.
Conversions
Falinwa: package price ÷ hours. Malt: €557 ÷ 7 hours ≈ €80/hour; the 7-hour day is an assumption.
Tax & scope
HT prices exclude VAT; other prices retain the source’s stated basis. Unspecified extras are excluded; features are not equivalent.
