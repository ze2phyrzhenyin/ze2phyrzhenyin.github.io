REX pitch: backup kit

Script, static slides and animated deck for the 3-minute English pitch of REX Digital & AI. If the venue computer fails, present from this page.

On stage

  1. Animated deck in this browser, below: press Full screen. Space, → or the clicker moves one step; ← goes back one slide.
  2. With PowerPoint: rex-pitch.pptx. With Keynote or an older PowerPoint: rex-pitch-compat.pptx.
  3. Any other computer: rex-pitch-static.pdf or rex-pitch-static.pptx. Pictures only, no animation; they open anywhere. One page per click, so the clicks match the animated deck.

If nothing can be clicked: rex-pitch.mp4 plays by itself, timed to the speech.

Playback in PowerPoint has not been tested on our machines. Rehearse once on the venue computer.

Script

3-minute limit. 262 words, about 2 min 20 s at a calm pace. ▸ marks one click.

  1. Slide 1REX Digital & AI

    We help businesses in traditional industries modernize their operations with digital and AI solutions.

  2. Slide 2Excel, email, paper

    Today, many of these businesses still rely on Excel, emails and manual processes.

    ▸Off-the-shelf software often fails to meet their specific needs.

  3. Slide 3One platform

    For example, we developed an intelligent management system for an international electronics warehousing company.

    ▸It brings work orders, inventory, smartphone testing and billing into one platform.

    ▸We even built their own calendar and email system.

    ▸It's already in daily use.

  4. Slide 4Every business is different

    But every business is different,

    ▸especially in manufacturing, agriculture and logistics.

    ▸With every project, we get faster at understanding complex needs and turning them into simple, intuitive tools.

  5. Slide 5Learning new software

    And yet, even with this system, some of our client's own customers still find learning new software frustrating and time-consuming.

  6. Slide 6The swap

    So we want to go one step further.

    ▸What if people no longer had to adapt to software?

    ▸What if software adapted to them?

  7. Slide 7Three interfaces, one system

    Imagine a retailer placing an order by talking to an AI assistant.

    ▸A logistics company receives it on its dashboard.

    ▸And the accountant keeps working in Excel.

    ▸Three different interfaces. One connected system. Everyone works in their own way, and they still work together.

    ▸That's the idea behind the new product we're building now.

  8. Slide 8The market

    According to France Num's 2026 survey, about one in four French small businesses say buying or upgrading software is a priority for the next two years.

    ▸That's roughly 600,000 companies.

  9. Slide 9Every business

    Our vision is to make digital transformation easy for every business, whatever its industry or digital skills.

  10. Slide 10Close

    Because we believe software should adapt to people, not the other way around.

Sources

Baromètre France Num 2026, Direction générale des Entreprises, report dated 2 October 2026, PDF in French.

  • Report page 6: about 2.4 million SMEs (TPE PME) in France; 9,655 businesses answered the survey.
  • Report page 72: 26% list « acquérir ou améliorer mes solutions numériques (logiciels) » (acquire or improve their software) among their digital priorities for the next two years. Base: all 9,655 respondents.
  • Slide 8: 600,000 is our estimate, 26% × 2.4 million. France Num does not publish this figure.
  • Slide 3: figures from the client’s production database, read on 26 November 2026: 7,971 work orders, 7,203 phone tests, 2,182 work orders in the last 30 days. The client is not named.
