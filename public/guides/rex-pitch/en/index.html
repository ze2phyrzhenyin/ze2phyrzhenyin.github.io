REX pitch: backup kit

Script, static slides and animated deck for the 3-minute English pitch of REX Digital & AI. If the venue computer fails, present from this page.

Version 3 · uploaded 2026-11-26 07:55 (Paris time)

On stage

  1. Animated deck in this browser, below: press Full screen, then Space to start. Each slide plays its animation by itself; when you are done with a slide, Space, → or the clicker moves to the next one, ← goes back one.
  2. With PowerPoint: rex-pitch-v3.pptx. Each slide plays its animation by itself; one click moves to the next slide. With Keynote or an older PowerPoint: rex-pitch-v3-compat.pptx.
  3. Any other computer: rex-pitch-v3-static.pdf or rex-pitch-v3-static.pptx. Pictures only, no animation; they open anywhere. In the .pptx the pictures of one slide follow each other on the speech timing and one click moves to the next slide; the PDF is turned by hand.

If nothing can be clicked: rex-pitch-v3.mp4 plays by itself, timed to the speech.

Playback in PowerPoint has not been tested on our machines. Rehearse once on the venue computer.

Full script

The final script as running text, one paragraph per slide, for reading through and rehearsing.

Good morning, everyone. I'm Zhaoyang, co-founder of REX Digital & AI.

Even in the age of AI, many traditional businesses still rely on Excel and manual processes because off-the-shelf software doesn't fit their needs. That's why we're here. We help these businesses modernize their operations through digital and AI solutions.

For example, we developed an intelligent management system for an international electronics warehousing company. It brings work orders, inventory, smartphone testing and billing into one platform. We even built their own calendar and email system. It's already in daily use.

But every business is different, especially in manufacturing, agriculture and logistics. With every project, we get faster at understanding complex needs and turning them into simple, intuitive tools.

And yet, even with this system, some of our client's own customers still find learning new software frustrating and time-consuming.

So we want to go one step further. What if people no longer had to adapt to software? What if software adapted to them?

Imagine a retailer placing an order by talking to an AI assistant. A logistics company receives it on its dashboard. And the accountant keeps working in Excel. Three different interfaces. One connected system. Everyone works in their own way, and they still work together. That's the idea behind the new product we're building now.

According to France Num's 2026 survey, about one in four French small businesses say buying or upgrading software is a priority for the next two years. That's roughly 600,000 companies.

Our vision is to make digital transformation easy for every business, whatever its industry or digital skills.

Because we believe software should adapt to people, not the other way around.
