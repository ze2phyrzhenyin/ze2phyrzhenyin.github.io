03 / Before you go

Tickets & arrival

Six practical rules for attending events. This page does not resell tickets and will never disguise third-party resale links as official sources.

01

Buy from official sources only

Prefer the organiser, venue, club or their explicitly authorised ticket partners. If sold out, wait for official returns or resale — never transfer money to unknown accounts.

02

Match dates may be set later

TFC and Stade Toulousain weekend fixtures often announce the opponent first, then confirm the date and kick-off time once broadcast schedules are set. Double-check before buying and before leaving.

03

Stadium / Ernest-Wallon

For popular matches, arrive at least 45–60 minutes early. Only buy public home sections; for security checks and post-match congestion, public transport is usually more reliable than ride-hailing.

04

Getting back from Le Bikini

The venue is in Ramonville. For late shows, check metro and shuttle last services in advance; arrange a carpool if needed. Bring ID for 18+ events.

05

Classical, opera & ballet

Arrive 25 minutes early at Halle aux Grains and Capitole. On a budget, prioritise centre sections first, then compare levels and sightlines.

06

Major shows at Zénith

Allow extra time for queuing and dispersal. On the day, check bag policies, prohibited items and entry instructions; recalculate your route with Tisséo.
