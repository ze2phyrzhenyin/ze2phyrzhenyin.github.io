03 / TICKETS

Buying logic

The ticket purchase order you really need

Being away from home does not mean that you have to go to the visiting team area. For you, the core is to determine whether the home team is open to general public sales, whether membership or ticket purchase history is required, and whether there are official return tickets.

01 / PRIMARY

Home team’s official starting lineup

First purchase on the home team’s official website or official app. Pages must be clearly public-facing and not enter visitors, away, parcage or supporter allocations.

02 / RESALE

Official secondary market

After the first sale is sold out, priority will be given to the club’s official return platform. Paris uses Ticketplace; other clubs release seats to season ticket holders according to their respective account systems.

03 / HOSPITALITY

Official Hospitality

For games with strict qualifications or high demand such as Manchester City, Villa, Paris-Barcelona, official VIP tickets are the most reliable guarantee, but they are usually significantly higher than ordinary tickets.

04 / HOME STAND

Behavior in the home team area

Wear neutral clothing, no Manchester City, Barcelona or PSG scarves and no provocative celebrations in the home team’s core area. It is clear that home fans only tickets cannot be understood as neutral zones.

About price:The following are the planning ranges for an adult ordinary ticket, and are not the official price list published by the club. Actual price depends on seat map, dynamic pricing, membership stage and returning inventory; total budget includes transportation and lodging.

Does an ordinary home-club ticket guarantee entry as a neutral spectator?

No. The risk is lower only when the ticketed area is open to the public, away supporters are not explicitly excluded, and you follow the home section’s clothing and conduct rules. Clubs such as City and Villa may restrict ordinary tickets to home-club members or accounts with a purchase history.

When should I book flights?

You can reserve a hotel with free cancellation first. Commit to non-refundable transport only after confirming your ticket eligibility and visa arrangements. For Baku, wait for the final stadium confirmation; for UK matches, confirm both visa requirements and ticket eligibility first.

Why is Paris–Barcelona counted only once in the budget?

The match at Parc des Princes on 20 October is both a Barcelona away fixture and a Paris home fixture. It appears in both teams’ schedules, but requires only one match ticket and one trip to Paris.

Why is Barcelona–Manchester City also counted only once?

The match at Spotify Camp Nou on 8 December is both a Manchester City away fixture and a Barcelona home fixture. Each team’s schedule lists it, while the budget includes it only once, in the Barcelona group.

What about third-party resale tickets?

They carry substantially more risk than official channels, especially with dynamic QR codes, named tickets, Passo, the Villa App or the City App. Try the official initial sale, official returned tickets or resale, and official hospitality in that order. Other channels may leave you with an invalid ticket or refused entry.

Which three matches should I choose?

For a balanced selection: Barcelona–Manchester City, Paris–Barcelona and Sporting CP–Barcelona. To reduce the budget, replace Paris–Barcelona with Paris–Bratislava or Barcelona–Como.
