Hiring | International Business & Information Assistant Intern

Responsibilities

  1. Carry out daily information gathering, document organisation, filing and basic analysis.
  2. Collect and organise materials in Chinese and foreign languages, including academic literature, competitor and industry intelligence.
  3. Help identify potential clients, compile leads and conduct initial outreach.
  4. Support marketing activities such as social-media content curation, copywriting assistance and channel research.
  5. Maintain day-to-day communication with clients and overseas colleagues to advance routine tasks.
  6. Receive work assignments on a regular basis and report on progress, issues and interim results.
  7. Learn and adopt various tools as the business requires to improve work efficiency.

Requirements

  1. Basic skills in information retrieval, document management and file processing.
  2. Willingness to learn new tools and apply them to information gathering, content curation, translation support and market analysis.
  3. Ability to read and organise foreign-language materials; comfortable using translation tools for basic English or French information processing.
  4. Familiarity with common social-media platforms and general internet literacy.
  5. Good communication skills; able to interact with clients and colleagues clearly, politely and promptly.
  6. Emotionally stable and responsible; able to work independently towards well-defined goals.
  7. Responsive during working hours; willing to handle occasional high-intensity sprints when needed.
  8. Advance notice required for leave or special circumstances; proper handover must be ensured.

Nice to have

  1. Experience in marketing, client development, international business or social-media operations.
  2. Strong English or other foreign-language skills.
  3. Keen information sensitivity; able to distil actionable insights from large volumes of material.

Who we are looking for

You do not need to know everything from day one, but you should learn fast, execute steadily, communicate clearly and deliver reliably. We do not value performative busyness, but when the task demands it, we expect you to respond promptly, get into gear quickly and drive things to completion.

Position details

Location: Room 707, Building 2A, Skyworth Innovation Valley, Songbai Road, Shiyan Sub-district, Bao'an District, Shenzhen, Guangdong, China

Compensation: CNY 150--250 per day

Working hours: 9 am -- 6 pm; at least 3 days on-site per week, 1--2 days remote.

How to apply

Send your CV to zephyr2515@gmail.com and include your earliest available start date.
