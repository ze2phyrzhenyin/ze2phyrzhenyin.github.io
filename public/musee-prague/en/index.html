Prague Museums & Venues Guide (compiled by ChatGPT)

Tip: Opening hours and temporary closures may change. Always check the official website before your visit. The Klementinum usually requires booking a specific guided-tour time slot; most other venues do not require reservations for general visits.

Venue Information

VenueAddressOpening HoursBookingHighlightsWebsite
National Museum
Národní muzeum		 Václavské náměstí 68, 110 00 Praha 1 Daily 10:00–18:00, including Mondays. No time-slot reservation needed for general visits. Tickets can be purchased online or on site. E-tickets are valid for a single entry within 30 days of purchase. Highly recommended for a first visit to Prague. Features the historic building, Pantheon, Czech history, natural history, a whale skeleton, and an underground passage connecting the old and new buildings. https://www.nm.cz/en/visit-us/buildings/museum-complex-of-the-national-museum
Jewish Museum in Prague
Jewish Museum in Prague		 Visitor centre: Maiselova 15, 110 00 Praha 1
Sites spread across the Josefov quarter		 1 May – 31 Aug usually Sun–Fri 09:00–19:00; closed on Saturdays and Jewish holidays. Shorter hours in other seasons. No time-slot reservation needed for general visits. A combined "Prague Jewish Town" ticket is available in advance. Not a single building but a group of synagogues, exhibition halls and the Old Jewish Cemetery, including the Spanish Synagogue, Old-New Synagogue and Pinkas Synagogue. https://www.jewishmuseum.cz/en/info/visit/
Klementinum
Klementinum		 Mariánské náměstí 190/5, 110 00 Praha 1 Usually daily 09:00–20:00; entry by guided-tour time slot. Advance booking recommended. You need a timed ticket for an English or Czech guided tour; group sizes are limited. Best known for its Baroque Library, Meridian Hall and Astronomical Tower. The tour lasts about 50 minutes and climbing the tower involves roughly 172 steps. https://prague.eu/en/objevujte/the-clementinum-astronomical-tower-and-baroque-library/
National Technical Museum
Národní technické muzeum		 Kostelní 1320/42, 170 00 Praha 7 Tue–Sun 09:00–18:00; closed on Mondays. If Monday is a public holiday, usually open 09:00–18:00. No reservation needed for general visits. Tickets available on site or online. The mine exhibition requires a separate ticket. Great for fans of technology, industry, railways and transport. The main hall houses planes, cars and trains, with additional sections on astronomy, photography, printing and architecture. https://www.ntm.cz/en/for-visitors/opening-hours/main-building-in-prague
National Gallery Prague: Trade Fair Palace
National Gallery Prague · Trade Fair Palace		 Dukelských hrdinů 47, 170 00 Praha 7 Tue–Sun 10:00–18:00; closed on Mondays. No reservation required for permanent exhibitions. Tickets can be bought online or on site, per building or exhibition. Ideal for modern and contemporary art, including Czechoslovak art. The building itself is a landmark of Prague functionalist architecture. https://www.ngprague.cz/en/about/buildings/trade-fair-palace
Museum of Decorative Arts
Museum of Decorative Arts in Prague		 17. listopadu 2, 110 00 Praha 1 Tue 10:00–20:00; Wed–Sun 10:00–18:00; closed on Mondays. No reservation needed for general visits. Tickets available on site or online. Foreign-language guided tours must be arranged in advance. Great for lovers of design, glass, jewellery, furniture, fashion and graphic arts. The exhibition spaces are generally quiet. https://www.upm.cz/en/visiting/opening-hours/
DOX Centre for Contemporary Art
DOX Centre for Contemporary Art		 Poupětova 1, 170 00 Praha 7 Tue–Sun 11:00–19:00; closed on Mondays. No reservation needed for regular exhibitions. Some performances, talks and special events require separate tickets. Focuses on contemporary, experimental and socially engaged art -- not a traditional gallery. The rooftop Gulliver airship is one of the venue's icons. https://www.dox.cz/en/visit-us

Prague Historic Buildings & Landmarks Guide (compiled by ChatGPT)

Tip: Prague Castle, churches and government buildings may adjust their opening hours due to services, state events or maintenance. Check the official website on the day of your visit.

Historic Buildings

Building / ComplexAddressOpening HoursBookingHighlightsWebsite
Prague Castle
Prague Castle		 Hradčany, 119 08 Praha 1 Castle grounds daily 06:00–22:00. Historic buildings: 1 Apr – 31 Oct 09:00–17:00; 1 Nov – 31 Mar 09:00–16:00. St Vitus Cathedral usually opens after 12:00 on Sundays. Castle grounds are free to enter. Combined tickets for historic buildings are available at the visitor centre or online. Guided tours should be booked in advance; check for temporary closures on the day. Allow 3–4 hours. A logical route: St Vitus Cathedral, Old Royal Palace, St George's Basilica, Golden Lane. Arrive early in the morning to avoid midday tour-group crowds. https://www.hrad.cz/en/prague-castle-for-visitors
Old Town Square & Old Town Hall
Old Town Square & Old Town Hall		 Staroměstské náměstí 1/3, 110 00 Praha 1 The square is open around the clock. Old Town Hall: Jan–Mar daily 10:00–19:00; Apr–Dec daily 09:00–20:00. The Astronomical Clock apostle procession usually runs hourly 08:00–23:00. No reservation needed to visit the square. Tower and interior tickets are available on site or online; interior tours run at scheduled times. Best visited before 09:00 or after 19:00 when crowds thin out. Allow 1.5–2 hours for the tower, historic interiors and underground spaces. https://prague.eu/en/objevujte/old-town-hall-with-astronomical-clock-staromestska-radnice-s-orlojem/
Charles Bridge & Bridge Towers
Charles Bridge & Bridge Towers		 Karlův most, Praha 1
Connects Staré Město and Malá Strana		 The bridge is open 24/7 and free. Bridge towers: Jun–Sep usually 09:00–20:30; shorter hours in other months. No reservation needed to cross the bridge. Tower tickets are available on site or online; no fixed time slot required. Best around sunrise or after 21:00. Start from the Old Town Bridge Tower, cross over, then continue through Malá Strana and Kampa Island. https://prague.eu/en/objevujte/charles-bridge-karluv-most/
https://prague.eu/en/objevujte/old-town-bridge-tower-staromestska-mostecka-vez/
Vyšehrad
Vyšehrad		 V Pevnosti 163/2, 128 00 Praha 2 Outdoor ramparts, parks and walkways are accessible year-round. The visitor centre is usually open daily around 10:00–18:00 with a short midday break; underground passages and indoor sites follow specific schedules. No reservation needed for outdoor walks. Tickets for underground passages, Gorlice Hall or guided tours can be purchased at the visitor centre or checked on the website. Quieter than the Old Town. Allow 2–3 hours. Key sights: ramparts, Basilica of St Peter and St Paul, Vyšehrad Cemetery, and Vltava river views. https://praha-vysehrad.cz/en/visit/
Strahov Monastery & Library
Strahov Monastery & Library		 Strahovské nádvoří 132/1, 118 00 Praha 1 Daily 09:00–17:00; ticket office usually closes at 16:15, last entry at 16:30. Some holidays may bring closures or shorter hours. Tickets available online or on site. General visitors can only view the two historic library halls from the doorway; special guided tours are limited in number. Pairs well with Prague Castle in the same area. Allow 60–90 minutes. Key sights: Theological Hall, Philosophical Hall and the Cabinet of Curiosities. https://www.strahovskyklaster.cz/en/for-visitors/what-you-can-visit/strahov-library
Municipal House & Powder Gate
Municipal House & Powder Gate		 Municipal House: náměstí Republiky 1090/5, Praha 1
Powder Gate: náměstí Republiky 5, Praha 1		 Municipal House interior tours run at posted times; the ticket office is usually open daily 10:00–19:00. Powder Gate: Jun–Sep usually 09:00–20:30; shorter hours in other months. A guided tour is required to see the Municipal House interiors; book in advance. The Powder Gate generally does not require a fixed time slot. The two are right next to each other. The Municipal House is notable for its Art Nouveau interiors and Smetana Hall; the Powder Gate offers views over the Old Town. Allow about 1.5–2 hours total. https://www.obecnidum.cz/en/tours/
https://prague.eu/en/objevujte/powder-gate-tower-prasna-brana/
Waldstein Palace & Garden
Waldstein Palace & Garden		 Palace: Valdštejnské náměstí 4, 118 01 Praha 1
Garden entrance: Letenská, Praha 1		 Garden open Apr–Oct: weekdays 07:00–19:00; weekends and Czech public holidays 09:00–19:00. Palace usually open to the public on Saturdays 09:00–16:00, Apr–Oct. Individual visits to the garden or palace open days are usually free and do not require reservation. Groups of 10+ should contact the venue at least two weeks in advance. Pairs well with Malá Strana and Charles Bridge. The garden features a grotto wall, Sala Terrena, fountains and peacocks. Allow 45–60 minutes. https://www.senat.cz/informace/pro_verejnost/valdstejnska_zahrada/index-eng.php
https://www.senat.cz/informace/pro_verejnost/prohlidky_senatu/otevreni_valdstejnskeho_palace-eng.php

Prague Bookshops Guide (compiled by ChatGPT)

Tip: No reservation is needed for any of these bookshops. Shops marked "second-hand" or "incl. second-hand" are good for hunting old books. Many traditional antikvariáts are closed at weekends -- check opening hours before you go.
